Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Splitsy iPhone app ("Splitsy", "we") collects, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short and collect only what the app needs.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Wouter Altepost, a private individual in the Netherlands. Contact: wjaltepost@gmail.com.

Your location is used on your phone to show nearby bars. It is sent to our servers only when you look up bars near you or score a scan. Reminders for plans are local notifications on your phone. Splitsy has no ads, no tracking and no analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data.

Who can see what

  • Your username, display name and profile photo are visible to other Splitsy users (for example in search).
  • Your scans, photos and stats are visible to you, to members of crews you share, and to people whose follow request you accepted.
  • Someone you blocked, or who blocked you, doesn't see your scans.
  • Founders (moderators) can see reported content to review it.

Service providers

We use these providers to run Splitsy. They process data only on our instructions, under data processing terms.

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage and server functions), hosted in the EU (Ireland).
  • Anthropic (USA): each scan photo is sent to Anthropic's AI model to measure the split. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, and Apple Maps and Look Around, which the app loads directly from Apple.
  • Google (Google Places): we look up a bar by its name and location to show its photos; the photos load directly from Google, which sees your device's IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap and Anthropic web search: to find bars. No personal data is involved.
  • Google (Gmail): the email that tells a founder about a new report contains the usernames involved and the report.

Where data goes to the USA, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, scans and photos: until you delete your account (in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account). Deleting removes them for good, and revokes Sign in with Apple.
  • Rounds and past plans in a crew stay as crew history, without your name.
  • Reports about you are deleted with your account; reports you made stay without your name.
  • Content removed by a moderator is deleted right away.
  • Our hosting provider keeps backups for a short time, after which deleted data is gone from them too.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app (Edit profile, Delete account). For anything else, email wjaltepost@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in your country.

Age

Splitsy is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn of it, we delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links to people allowed to see them.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we'll tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
