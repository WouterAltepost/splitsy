Common questions

I don't get my sign-in code

Check your spam folder; the email comes from "Splitsy" with the subject "Your Splitsy code". Codes work for one hour. You can also use Continue with Apple.

How is a split scored?

Splitsy looks at your photo and measures where the line between the stout and the head sits compared to the middle of the G on the glass. 100 is a perfect split. Measuring is automated and can be off on a blurry, dark or tilted photo: hold the phone upright, level with the G, with the whole glass in frame.

Why can't I scan again yet?

After each scored split there's a 20-minute pause, and there are at most 30 attempts per night. Splitsy rewards a good split, not more pints.

"Scoring is paused"

Scoring has a monthly budget. When it's used up, scoring pauses until the 1st of the next month. Nothing you scored before is lost.

Crews, rounds and prizes

A crew plays a running leaderboard in rounds. A prize is a private agreement between friends; Splitsy doesn't run, hold or pay out prizes.

Reporting and blocking

Use the ⋯ menu on a split, a plan, a crew's info screen or someone's profile to report it. Reported content is hidden for you right away, and a founder reviews every report within 24 hours. Blocking someone (⋯ on their profile) hides their splits, plans and duels from you, also in crews you share. Manage blocks in Profile → Settings → Blocked people.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, scans and photos for good. If you signed in with Apple, Splitsy's access to your Apple ID is revoked too. Can't open the app? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it.

Location

Splitsy uses your location only while you use the app: to show bars near you and to check that a scan was made at the bar you picked. You can turn it off in iOS Settings; the map then starts in Europe and scans count as "not at a bar".
